# AkuBin recycling and rewards

Marketplace verification is not required to use AkuBin. Sign in to associate your
PIN, deposits and points with your account. Points are separate from Marketplace money.

Buyers, sellers, Buyer & Seller accounts and logistics partners can all use AkuBin.
AkuBin always appears in every account. There is no signup or Settings toggle.
Choose Recycle & Earn at signup to make AkuBin your main workspace.

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Public AkuBin / bin locations | Find a bin, accepted materials, opening hours and reported availability. |
| Bin detail / QR link | Identify the physical bin and see its location and instructions. The website does not unlock the bin or create a deposit. |
| My AkuBin (`/app/akubin`) | View or change your PIN, account totals, deposits, session history and points ledger. Expand a session for its backend status; end an active session if necessary. |
| Rewards (`/app/akubin/rewards`) | Browse available rewards, review current earning rules, submit a redemption and track its status. |
| Leaderboard (`/app/akubin/leaderboard`) | Compare server-recorded points for the selected period. |
| Bin map (`/app/akubin/map`) | Find server-provided bin locations. Availability depends on received telemetry. |

1. Find an available bin and check the accepted materials.
2. Use your account PIN on the physical keypad. The controller asks the backend to
   start a session. Keep your PIN private.
3. Insert an item as the hardware instructs. The camera classifies it and the load
   cells measure its weight. The controller reports the deposit to the backend.
4. The backend validates the event and applies its configured reward rules. Refresh
   My AkuBin to check the recorded deposit and points. A hardware display alone is
   not proof that the backend accepted a deposit.
5. Finish on the bin, or end an active account session from My AkuBin.

Choose a reward only when you have enough points and the reward has stock. A request
reserves points and becomes pending review. Rejection returns the reserved points;
approval means it is awaiting fulfilment. Completed means staff recorded an existing
fulfilment receipt. This administrative action does not itself send cash or airtime.
Cash and airtime thresholds come from the backend's current rules.

Use Support for an unrecorded deposit or reward problem. Include the bin code,
approximate time and session/deposit ID if available. Do not submit repeated fake
deposits from the browser. Hardware integration and AI availability are separate
from frontend page validation.
# Join for recycling only

Choose **Recycle & Earn — use AkuBin** during signup, confirm your email and finish
account setup. Your home is **My AkuBin**. You can set a PIN, see deposits and points,
find bins and request rewards without choosing Buyer, Seller or Logistics and without
Marketplace verification. AkuBin is included automatically for this account choice.

Marketplace browsing remains available. To focus on buying, selling or delivery,
change your account role in Settings and complete the relevant verification or
partner approval. Your recycling PIN and points remain tied to the same account.
