# Logistics partner workflow

Use **Logistics partner** (`/app/partner`) to apply, review jobs, submit quotes and
manage assigned deliveries. Partner approval is separate from Marketplace verification.

Choose Logistics Partner at signup for this workspace. You can also enable AkuBin
independently; recycling is available to delivery partners as well as buyers/sellers.
Choosing the logistics role does not create or approve the business application.

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Application | Submit business/contact details, service areas, materials, capacity and private documents. |
| Application status | See pending, approved, rejected or suspended status. Rejected applications can be resubmitted; suspension requires staff support. |
| Available / recommended jobs | Eligible requests matching the approved partner's service coverage and capacity. |
| Quotes | Offer a logistics fee and delivery estimate, or decline a job. A submitted quote is not an assignment. |
| Deliveries | Orders where the buyer selected this partner; progress actions depend on backend order state. |
| Delivery proof | Upload actual evidence for a completed delivery and submit its upload ID. |

The application requires owned, ready uploads for business registration (CAC),
owner identification, vehicle registration and insurance. Supply the real supported
capacity and service areas. Staff review the application before jobs become available.

For an eligible job, inspect the material, quantity and route, then submit a quote.
The buyer selects among quotes. Updating a quote does not create another assignment.
Declining removes the job from your current eligibility; the buyer can reopen quoting.
Buyer and seller cannot quote as the independent delivery partner for their own order.

After selection, wait for verified payment before pickup. Record pickup, transit and
delivery in order, then upload proof. Buyer inspection, confirmation or dispute comes
next. Independent finance staff handle settlement. Approved partners can configure
a payout recipient without Marketplace full verification, but payout setup and
execution depend on Paystack and are not currently validated end to end.

For failed payment, a suspended account or a disputed delivery, use Support. Do not
advance physical delivery states to bypass a payment failure. The frontend follows
the backend's allowed transitions.
